OUR BUILDING OVERVIEW

THE HIGH PREMIUM QUALITY LUXURY APARTMENTS

Adhering to values of trustworthiness, honesty and architectural excellence.

Hanco Property Developers Pvt. Ltd is a pioneer in delivering quality living spaces adhering to values of trustworthiness and honesty. Established in 2011, Hanco has been building apartments that are uniquely stylish and functional with beautiful interiors, relaxed outdoors, vibrant neighborhood and unparalleled amenities, offering customers a life of comfort and convenience.

So far, Hanco has executed many projects within Kerala to the complete satisfaction of our trusted clientele. Customers have always been our priority and providing them with a perfect home that fits their routine, our passion. Right from the conceptual stage to execution, our expert multidisciplinary team makes sure that our customers’ dreams are realized and strive hard to make their home journey enjoyable.
