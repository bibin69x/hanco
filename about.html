Who We Are

Hanco Property Developers Pvt. Ltd is a pioneer in delivering quality living spaces adhering to values of trustworthiness and honesty. Established in 2011, Hanco has been building apartments that are uniquely stylish and functional with beautiful interiors, relaxed outdoors, vibrant neighborhood and unparalleled amenities, offering customers a life of comfort and convenience.

So far, Hanco has executed many projects within Kerala to the complete satisfaction of our trusted clientele. Customers have always been our priority and providing them with a perfect home that fits their routine, our passion.

Right from the conceptual stage to execution, our team, comprising experts in different areas of expertise, makes sure that our customers’ dreams are realized and strive hard to make their home journey enjoyable.

The Foundation

From H. Ananthanarayanan & Co to HANCO

Incorporated on March 31, 2011 (CIN: U45200KL2011PTC028080) under the Registrar of Companies, Ernakulam, Hanco Property Developers emerged from the engineering foundation of the HANCO Group (H. Ananthanarayanan & Co).

Established in the historic heritage precinct of Kalpathy, the company has stayed intentionally focused on Palakkad, mastering the microclimate, wind dynamics, and geological soil structures of the region rather than pursuing overstretched speculative development across disparate geographies.
