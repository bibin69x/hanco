Context & Heritage

Living Within Palakkad's Cultural Hearth

Vadakkanthara is famed for its quiet, tree-lined residential streets and historic temple traditions. Krishna Leela respects this delicate urban texture with a calibrated five-storey profile that sits harmoniously within its neighborhood.

The project incorporates an on-site Sewage Treatment Plant (STP), covered electric vehicle charging infrastructure, and a dual-filtration water reservoir drawing from both certified deep borewells and municipal water connections.
