## 1. Global Navigation Architecture

The site navigation is structured around an editorial header with clear information zoning:

```
[ LOGO: HANCO ] ----------------------------------------------------------- [ ACTIONS ]
  Primary Links:
  - HOME          (/)
  - PROJECTS      (/projects)
  - SERVICES      (/services)
  - DISCOVER ▾    (Refined Mega-Menu / Accordion)
  - ESTIMATE      (/estimate  - [ Highlighted Interactive Experience ])
  - CONTACT       (/contact)
```

### The DISCOVER Mega-Menu Blueprint
Clicking or hovering over **DISCOVER** reveals a three-column editorial layout:

* **Column 01: The Developer**
  * **Our Story (`/about#story`):** Established 2011 from H. Ananthanarayanan & Co; leadership by Ananthanarayanan Hariharan & Vani Subalakshmi.
  * **Our Philosophy (`/about#philosophy`):** Building for the Palakkad Gap climate; light, air, and enduring thermal comfort.
  * **How We Build (`/discover/how-we-build`):** Engineering standards, pile foundations, seismic safety, and multi-stage waterproofing.
* **Column 02: Standards & Palakkad**
  * **Quality & Craft (`/discover/quality`):** Tactile materiality, timber joinery, sound attenuation, premium electrical and sanitary fittings.
  * **Palakkad Locations (`/discover/locations`):** In-depth neighbourhood guides for Stadium Bypass Road, Kalpathy, Vadakkanthara, and Mattumantha.
  * **Homebuyer FAQs (`/discover/faqs`):** Essential answers for local and NRI purchasers (legal processes, bank approvals, possession timelines).
* **Column 03: Transparency & Contact**
  * **K-RERA & Documents (`/discover/rera`):** Direct public directory of all K-RERA certificates, sanctioned plans, and quarterly compliance logs.
  * **Kalpathy Headquarters (`/contact`):** Heritage office on Mani Iyer Road, direct contact lines, and private consultation bookings.

## 3. Cross-Linking & Conversion Architecture

To ensure fluid user movement without aggressive popups, the site employs natural architectural signposts:

```
[ HOME ] ------------> [ PROJECTS ] ------------> [ PROJECT DETAIL ]
   |                        |                            |
   |                        |                            v
   v                        |                  [ VIEW K-RERA DOCS ]
[ ESTIMATE TOOL ] <---------+                            |
   |                                                     v
   +-------------------------------------------> [ PRIVATE WALKTHROUGH ]
   |
   v
[ SERVICES ] --------> [ ABOUT / STORY ] -------> [ CONTACT & VISIT ]
```

1. **Estimate Tool as Anchor:** Accessible from every page via a distinguished header action, directing qualified leads to matching project details.
2. **K-RERA Transparency as Trust Anchor:** Every project detail page links directly to `/discover#rera`, allowing instant third-party validation on the official Kerala RERA portal.
3. **Contextual Footers:** Every page ends with a clean next-step signpost (e.g., from *Projects* to *Estimate*, from *About* to *Contact*).
