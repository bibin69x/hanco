Hanco Fort Heights

Architectural Narrative

Composed for Light and Western Ghats Breezes

Positioned along the wide Stadium Bypass Road adjacent to the Civil Station, Fort Heights provides uninterrupted visual access to the peaks of the Western Ghats. The dual-tower configuration was deliberately engineered to eliminate dark corridors, ensuring that every apartment enjoys dual-aspect natural light and cross-ventilation.

The building crowns its residential levels with a private recreational terrace featuring an infinity-edge swimming pool, acoustic screening theatre, and fully equipped health club.

Engineering Dossier

Technical Construction Specifications

Foundation & Structure Seismic Zone III compliant RCC framed structure on high-capacity certified pile foundations. Fe 500D high-ductility TMT steel reinforcement.
Superstructure & Masonry High-density solid concrete blocks with external sponge-finish plaster and anti-fungal acrylic weather coat. Acoustic isolation between common party walls.
Flooring & Finishes 800x800mm premium double-charged vitrified tiles for living, dining, and master suites. Anti-skid matte ceramic finishes on balconies and utility courtyards.
Joinery & Glazing Teakwood entrance door frame with polyurethane clear finish and high-security digital deadbolt. Heavy-duty 3-track UPVC sliding balcony windows with integrated stainless steel mosquito mesh.
Electrical & Backup Concealed multi-strand copper wiring (Finolex/Havells) with modular Legrand switches. 100% automatic diesel generator power backup for apartments, lifts, and common utilities.
Plumbing & Sanitary Concealed CPVC/PVC water lines. Water-saving dual-flush wall-hung EWC fixtures by Kohler/Jaquar with thermostatic diverters in master bathrooms.
EV & Environmental Systems Dedicated electric vehicle charging bays in covered stilt parking, central rainwater harvesting reservoir, and organic solid waste management.
